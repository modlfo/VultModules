The blank panels come in three sizes: 3 HP, 6 HP and 12 HP. The 6 HP and 12 HP versions have no function beyond making your Rack look more beautiful by adding the Vult logo.

## The 3 HP panel is not blank

The smallest panel hides a vector generator. It has **X** and **Y** outputs that, patched into a scope in X-Y mode, draw a logo. The shape is traced continuously rather than stored as an image, so it can be scanned slowly, stretched, or pushed to audio rate where it becomes a sound source of its own.

- **Speed**: how fast the shape is traced. At low settings the scope draws the outline slowly; turned up, the outputs reach audio rate.
- **X** and **Y** (knobs): scale the drawing on each axis, which stretches or squashes the shape.
- **X** and **Y** (outputs): the two coordinates. Both need to be patched for the drawing to make sense.

The outputs only run while something is connected to them, so an unpatched panel costs nothing.
